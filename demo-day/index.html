Demo Day - March 16th, 2026

Welcome to Demo Day - March 16th, 2026 for the Winter 2026 AR/VR Capstone!

Our students will present their AR/VR projects from this past quarter in the atrium of the Allen Center.

Headsets (Quest 3 and Apple Vision Pro) will be sanitized between every use.

Schedule

Time: Monday March 16th -- 12:30 - 2:30 PM Pacific Time
Location: Held in the Allen Center for Computer Science & Engineering
Description: Come try seven new AR/VR + AI applications and meet the student developers! Each project has a space in the Allen Atrium of the Allen Center and all applications were developed by the students of the 2026 AR/VR Capstone class

Team Projects

oVRbrewed

  • oVRbrewed! is a mixed reality coffee shop game where your physical space becomes the café. Place café stations on real surfaces, physically move between them to prepare drinks and food, and race to complete orders before time runs out. Watch out for hazards like fires and broken machines that can throw your kitchen into chaos!

Blubble

  • Blubble is an augmented reality conversation assistant built for Apple Vision Pro that helps users understand and remember conversations in real time. It shows live transcription, analyzes the emotional tone of speech, and generates quick context cards for topics mentioned during the conversation. The goal is to reduce cognitive load and make conversations easier, especially for people who struggle with tone interpretation or working memory.

BLUEVR

  • BLUEVR is an interactive baseball umpire simulator. If you’ve ever watched an MLB game, I’m sure you or someone around you has said something negative about the umpire behind home plate. This game is your chance to see if you could do better. It’s also just a great way to practice!

Reliv

  • Reliv is a multiplayer virtual reality experience for Meta Quest 3 that transforms your photos into immersive 3D worlds you can explore together. Using AI-powered 2D-to-3D scene generation, users can upload any image and step inside it as a living environment shared with friends in real time. Snap photos from within the experience using an in-world VR camera, hang out with custom avatars, and make any moment a place to be.

CoARL

  • Want to visit the Great Barrier Reef but can't? CoARL brings the Great Barrier Reef to you. Explore the reef in your own space and learn about the different coral and aquatic creatures that call the reef home.

Bubble Team

  • Bubble Team is a co-op VR game where you learn how to cook boba/toppings, make drinks, and take customer orders. To succeed, players will need to speak to customers in real time and work together to make drinks as fast as possible to maximize profit.

Hearth & Horde Lockdown

  • Hearth & Horde Lockdown is an AR/VR wave-defense survival game where players can change their space in their very own home base. With fast paced combat and base building management, the game helps players learn how to arrange, defend, and upgrade spaces to survive.
